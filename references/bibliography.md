# Bibliography

The full annotated list of sources is in **[curriculum/sources.md](curriculum/sources.md)**. It
covers textbooks, official documents and standards, university courses, peer-reviewed papers,
datasets and software. Each entry has a short key, its verification status, access notes, and
the lessons it supports. That page also has:

- the [minimum reading path](curriculum/sources.md?id=minimum-reading-path-13-items), 13 items that together cover the whole course;
- the list of [items and chapters to skip](curriculum/sources.md?id=items-and-chapters-to-skip) under the safety boundary;
- [free alternatives](curriculum/sources.md?id=paywalled-items-and-free-alternatives) for paywalled books.

Related references: [glossary](references/glossary.md) · [standards map](references/standards.md).
