# 03 — Detection, Post-Blast Forensics, and Case-Study Sources

Scope: an educational, graduate-level self-study reading list for a software/AI engineer. It covers sensing physics, how detectors are evaluated, how post-blast investigations are run as a science, and historical case outcomes. It contains no render-safe procedures and no device design details.

Research date: 2026-09-27.

Verification key:
- **VERIFIED**: the URL was fetched in this session (HTTP 200) and the title and content were confirmed.
- **VERIFIED (index)**: the URL showed up in search results on the official or publisher domain, but a direct fetch was bot-blocked (403). Bibliographic details come from search metadata.
- **UNVERIFIED**: the source was cited but its URL was not confirmed.
