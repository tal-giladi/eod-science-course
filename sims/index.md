# Simulators

Ten browser simulators. Each runs stand-alone (open it full-screen) and is embedded in the lessons it
supports. Every simulator has four difficulty levels (Beginner · Intermediate · Advanced · Expert),
a replayable seed (`?seed=…&level=…`), withholds the answer until you commit, and ends with a
debrief: *what you observed · what you decided · what you missed · what uncertainty existed · what
professional reasoning would consider · relevant physics · relevant engineering · links back to the
course.* Results are stored locally in your browser.

| | Simulator | What you do | Lessons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | [Scene Assessment (3D)](sims/scene-assessment/index.html ':ignore') | rotate a 3D scene, task a robot, inspect items with camera / imaging / trace, mark hazards, set cordon & control point, evacuate or shelter | 00.2, 07.1, 07.2 |
| B | [EOD Robot](sims/eod-robot/index.html ':ignore') | drive a tracked robot with delayed video, pan/tilt, thermal, 2-link arm; manage link budget, loss-of-comms behaviour, odometry drift | 06.1–06.6, 06.9 |
| C | [Sensor Fusion](sims/sensor-fusion/index.html ':ignore') | survey a field with five imperfect sensors under a budget; correlated errors; naive vs exact posterior; value of information | 05.1–05.6, 09.4 |
| D | [Blast Physics](sims/blast-physics/index.html ':ignore') | Friedlander gauge, scaled-distance curves, 2D Euler wave field (open, wall, corner, street, room), SDOF response & P–I diagram, prediction challenges | 01.3–01.6, 04.1–04.4 |
| E | [Post-Blast Investigation](sims/post-blast/index.html ':ignore') | scene safety, search, photograph/mark/collect with chain of custody, reconstruct seat and abstract yield from evidence | 08.1–08.3 |
| F | [Incident Command](sims/incident-command/index.html ':ignore') | a developing incident: request information, task sensors, isolate, escalate, decide when evidence is insufficient | 07.1, 07.2 |
| G | [Robotics Engineering](sims/robotics-engineering/index.html ':ignore') | write controllers & planners in JS: obstacle course, IK, relay placement, energy-optimal planning, pushing, noisy localisation | 06.3–06.9, 09.5 |
| H | [Recognition Trainer](sims/recognition-trainer/index.html ':ignore') | category-level recognition of generic synthetic illustrations; state (fired/unknown) and response category; calibration | 03.1–03.4 |
| I | [Shock Tube & Hugoniot](sims/shock-tube/index.html ':ignore') | normal-shock ratios, exact Riemann shock tube vs numerical scheme, reactive Hugoniot & CJ point | 01.3, 01.4, 02.2 |
| J | [Detection-Theory Lab](sims/detection-theory/index.html ':ignore') | ROC, base rates, costs, clearance economics, blind-trial evaluation with confidence bounds | 05.1, 05.7 |

<div class="callout boundary">

**Fiction by design.** All objects are generic and fictional; yields are abstract units; decision
simulators score information gathering, safety and reasoning under uncertainty — never procedures
against devices.
